DESIGN TARGET

Gargantua energy, browser budget.

This is not a physically exact Kerr black-hole simulation. The fragment shader deliberately cheats: it bends procedural stars, projects a turbulent accretion disk, mirrors luminous material around the photon sphere and crushes the center into a clean event horizon. The goal is the visual language of gravitational lensing while remaining fast enough to become the background of No Hope.
