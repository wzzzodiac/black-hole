ALPHA TARGET

Physics first. Pretty later.

V2 deliberately starts simpler than V1. The important part is the pipeline: per-pixel rays, iterative gravitational bending, event-horizon interception, equatorial-disk crossing and escaped background light. The current kernel uses dimensionless units and a browser-budget Schwarzschild-inspired null-ray equation; it is not yet a research-grade relativistic renderer.
